# Roadmap

What's shipped and what's next for ZE Laser Practice. Tick a box when it lands; add anything you defer.

## Shipped
A browser laser trainer in the spirit of cybo's lasers.cybo.dev (which is paused while he's away), writing his
exact map-folder format so he can lift our maps straight into his site. See [README.md](README.md).
- [x] **Fast exact ray traces** (2026-11-05) — zero-size traces walk the BVH along the ray (TriangleBVH.queryRay) and test triangles exactly (rayTriangle): ~97x faster (18 µs vs 1.8 ms per ray on mist), and on grazing rays the hit is within DIST_EPSILON of the true crossing (the box clip stopped up to 170 units short); box traces (movement) unchanged
- [x] **Ray traces hit thin triangles** (2026-11-05) — a zero-size trace missed nearly every triangle (DIST_EPSILON crossed enter and leave); fixed in world/collision.js clipSlab, so boss line-of-sight checks and the co-op anti-cheat's canHit no longer see through walls
